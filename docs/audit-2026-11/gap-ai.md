## What is already right
- OWASP LLM 2026 IDs and the 2025 mapping match the published list; AI Act dates match 2026/1744.
- `rubric`/`human` never count as passed; missing `tool_calls` is "not evaluated"; one failed run fails a critical case.
- REF §3: rule of three, no best-of-N retries, production temperature, paired comparison.
- Rubric template: human–human agreement first, false-pass direction, judge-injection rule.
- "Prompt defences are not controls", "test effects, not words"; the release decision stays with stakeholders.

## Not worth doing
- Neuron coverage or ML-training tooling: white-box, wrong audience.
- Calling models or bundling RAGAS/DeepEval in `ai_eval.py`: breaks stdlib-only and no-network.
- BLEU/ROUGE/embedding scorers: weak oracles.
- CT-GenAI prompt-pattern theory in SKILL bodies: prose that hurts weak models.
- Explainability (LIME) and energy sections: no change in agent output.
